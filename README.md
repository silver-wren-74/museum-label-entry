Entry for the [Museum of the Year 3000](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-museum-of-the-year-3000-b727a0bb)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one line, the museum label a
museum in the year 3000 puts beside an ordinary object from today.

`main.py` writes the label to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Wet Floor Sign, c. 2024. Placed after mopping, to warn visitors of the
> very danger the cleaners had just created — and left there for hours
> after it dried.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. The label is 155 characters, one line,
UTF-8.
